# gogo-terms-privacy-policy

Working documents and compliance tracking for the **GoGo Board** Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.

The GoGo Board platform is jointly operated by:

- Teaching and Learning Innovation Center (TLIC), Chiang Mai University — Thailand
- Transformative Learning Technologies Lab (TLTL), Teachers College, Columbia University — USA

## Status

Both documents are in **working-draft form** and are not yet published. Published versions will live at:

- Terms of Use: https://www.gogoboard.org/terms
- Privacy Policy: https://www.gogoboard.org/privacy

## Repository structure

### Camera-ready drafts (public-facing rendition)

- [`drafts/GoGo_Board_Terms_of_Use.md`](drafts/GoGo_Board_Terms_of_Use.md) — Terms of Use, v1.0 Draft 1, prose form
- [`drafts/GoGo_Board_Privacy_Policy.md`](drafts/GoGo_Board_Privacy_Policy.md) — Privacy Policy, v1.0 Draft 1, prose form

These are the public-facing renditions of the source documents — converted to prose, with scaffolding removed. They represent how the documents would look when published, still as "draft 1" pending final resolution of blocking items.

### Working files

All internal working files live under [`working-files/`](working-files/).

### Source documents (the drafts)

- [`GoGo_Board_Terms_of_Use_Source.md`](working-files/GoGo_Board_Terms_of_Use_Source.md) — Terms of Use source document
- [`GoGo_Board_Privacy_Policy_Source.md`](working-files/GoGo_Board_Privacy_Policy_Source.md) — Privacy Policy source document

These are bullet + label format working drafts. Before publication, they will be converted to prose and scaffolding will be stripped (see the "Pre-publication checklist" at the top of each source document).

### Compliance checklists (operational tasks before publication)

- [`Terms_of_Use_Compliance_Checklist.md`](working-files/Terms_of_Use_Compliance_Checklist.md) — operational tasks the ToU commits us to
- [`Privacy_Policy_Compliance_Checklist.md`](working-files/Privacy_Policy_Compliance_Checklist.md) — operational tasks the Privacy Policy commits us to

### Task assignments

- [`Tasks_for_Paulo.md`](working-files/Tasks_for_Paulo.md) — DMCA designated-agent setup tasks for the TLTL / Columbia side

## Workflow

1. Iterate on the source documents based on feature decisions and policy choices.
2. Track operational and capability requirements in the compliance checklists.
3. Assign specific items to the people responsible.
4. Before publication: resolve all BLOCKING items in the checklists, run the pre-publication strip pass, then post to the public URLs above.
