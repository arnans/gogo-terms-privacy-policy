## 1. Provide a phone number for the DMCA Designated Agent

**What:** A direct phone number that can be published in the ToU and registered with the US Copyright Office.

**Why:** US copyright law (DMCA, 17 USC §512) requires online platforms to designate an agent to receive copyright infringement notices. To qualify for the "safe harbor" protection against third-party claims, the agent's name, address, email, **and phone** must be:

- Listed in our public Terms of Use
- Registered with the US Copyright Office's DMCA Designated Agent Directory

Without this, the safe harbor doesn't apply, and the platform remains liable for any copyrighted material a user uploads.

**How:** Send us a phone number you're comfortable publishing. Your TLTL office line, a general TLTL number, or any reachable line works.

**Urgency:** Needed before we publish the ToU.

## 4. Establish a simple internal DMCA procedure

**What:** A short internal workflow for handling DMCA notices when they arrive.

**Why:** The ToU commits us to responding to notices and counter-notices. We need a defined process so the response is consistent and on time.

**How:** Suggested workflow — feel free to adapt. When a notice arrives at `dmca@gogoboard.org`:

1. **Verify the notice contains the required elements:**
   - Identification of the copyrighted work
   - Identification of the infringing material and its location on the platform
   - Contact info of the complainant
   - Statement of good-faith belief that the use is not authorized
   - Statement under penalty of perjury that the notice is accurate and that the sender is authorized
   - Physical or electronic signature
2. **If the notice is valid:** remove or disable access to the material, email the uploader to tell them the content was removed and on what basis, and log the action.
3. **If the notice is invalid** (missing elements): reply to the complainant explaining what's missing, and take no removal action.
4. **If a counter-notice arrives** from the uploader: forward it to the original complainant, then wait 10–14 business days. If no lawsuit is filed against the uploader in that window, restore the content.
5. **Track repeat infringers:** if the same user is the subject of multiple valid notices, their account may be terminated under ToU §18 (repeat-infringer policy).

A simple shared log (spreadsheet or document) listing the date, complainant, alleged work, action taken, and outcome is sufficient.

**Urgency:** Needed before we publish the ToU. Can be a short document.
