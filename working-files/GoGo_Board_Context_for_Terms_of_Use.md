# GoGo Board – Context Information for Terms of Use Generation

**Purpose of this document**  
This document provides non-legal context about how the GoGo Board platform is intended to be used.  
It helps an AI system or writer generate a clear and appropriate Terms of Use document.

---

## 1. Nature of the Platform

The GoGo Board platform is an **educational tool**, not a consumer social platform.

It supports:
- Classroom-based learning
- Project-based STEM education
- Experimentation with hardware and software

Use is typically supervised by teachers or institutions.

---

## 2. Intended Use

The platform is designed for:
- Learning
- Teaching
- Educational experimentation

It is **not intended** for:
- Commercial deployment
- Safety-critical systems
- Industrial or medical use

---

## 3. Educational Responsibility Model

- Teachers guide and supervise student use
- Schools define acceptable behavior and discipline
- The platform provides tools, not instruction guarantees

---

## 4. User Behavior Expectations

Expected behavior includes:
- Respectful collaboration
- Academic integrity
- Safe handling of hardware
- Compliance with school rules

---

## 5. Hardware & Physical Projects

GoGo Board projects may involve:
- Electronics
- Moving parts
- Sensors and actuators

Assumptions:
- Users follow safety instructions
- Teachers supervise physical activities
- Schools provide appropriate environments

---

## 6. User-Generated Content

Users may create:
- Code
- Project documentation
- Data logs
- Media related to projects

This content is part of the learning process and varies in quality and accuracy.

---

## 7. Service Limitations

The platform:
- May change features over time
- May experience downtime
- Is provided to support learning, not guaranteed outcomes

---

## 8. Account Control in Schools

Common practices:
- Schools or teachers create and manage accounts
- Accounts may be suspended for policy violations
- Data access follows classroom roles

---

## 9. Intellectual Property Context

- Platform software and materials are owned by the operator
- Educational materials may be shared under specific licenses
- Student work remains student work

---

## 10. Liability Perspective (Non-Legal)

- Learning projects may fail or behave unexpectedly
- Physical projects carry inherent risk
- Responsibility is shared among users, teachers, and institutions

---

## 11. Geographic & Institutional Scope

The platform is used:
- In multiple countries
- In schools, universities, and workshops

Local rules and school policies often apply in addition to platform rules.

---

## 12. How This Document Should Be Used

This document:
- Provides intent and assumptions
- Does not define legal obligations
- Should be combined with verified operational details
